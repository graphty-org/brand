# graphty logo usage terms

The graphty name, the graphty logos (the mark, the wordmark and every lockup), the package logos
in `child-brands/` and the Cytoscape co-brand in `co-brand/` are trademarks of the graphty project
([graphty-org](https://github.com/graphty-org)). These terms say when you may use them without
asking.

## You may

- Use the logos to refer to graphty, to one of its packages, or to an integration with graphty:
  for example in a blog post, an article, a talk, a tutorial, a course, a list of tools, or a page
  that says your project is built with or works with graphty.
- Link to graphty, its documentation or its repository with a logo.
- Show a package's logo next to that package, for example in a README or a list of dependencies.
- Resize the logo files, keeping their proportions, and pick the version (color, white or black)
  that suits the background, as the [brand guide](README.md) describes.

## You may not

- Present graphty, or anything built from it, as your own product.
- Suggest that the graphty project sponsors, endorses, certifies or partners with you, your
  product or your organization.
- Change the logos: recolor, redraw, stretch, rotate, crop, animate, add effects to them, or
  combine them with other marks, words or artwork.
- Use a graphty logo, or anything confusingly similar to one, as part of your own logo, brand,
  product name, project name, app icon or domain name.
- Place a graphty logo more prominently than your own name or logo when you show both.
- Use the logos on merchandise you sell.

## The code license does not cover the logos

The graphty packages are open source under the MIT License (see each package's `LICENSE` file).
That license covers the code only. It grants no right to use the graphty name or logos as a
trademark; that is what these terms are for. Outfit, the typeface of the wordmark, has its own
license, in [fonts/OFL.txt](fonts/OFL.txt).

The Cytoscape.js logo inside the co-brand belongs to the Cytoscape Consortium, not to the graphty
project, and these terms grant nothing about it.

## Asking for other uses

For any use these terms do not allow, or if you are not sure, open an issue on
[graphty-org/brand](https://github.com/graphty-org/brand/issues) describing what you want
to do and where. Do not use the logos that way until the project agrees in writing.

The graphty project may update these terms, and may ask you to stop a use that does not follow
them.
