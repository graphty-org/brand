graphty logo, round 4: the graph marks

The non-letter marks. G1 to G3 rework the simple triangle graph (3G); G4 to G7 bring back the graph of four nodes and three edges with one node in the middle. Every mark is hand-built SVG from circles and straight lines; the accent node is cut out of the edges, so it still stands apart in one color. The colors are the three palettes picked in round 3: P1 ink + lime, P2 graphite + magenta, P3 indigo + amber.

Children for layout and algorithms (in both families): L2, the graph on x/y axes, and A2, the graph in round brackets like a function call f( ), stay readable at 16 px and pair with the angle brackets of graphty-element. A1, the lit route, also reads well but leans heavily on color. L3, the graph inside a circle, turns the y-shaped G5 into a peace sign or a car badge, so use it only with the triangle. L1, the dot grid, breaks up into noise at 16 px. L4, the corner arrow, reads as "resize" more than "layout". The lockups use Figtree for every package; the font comparison at the end of each family shows Figtree, Onest and Outfit, all SIL OFL 1.1.

These have not been shown to a panel of readers yet, so the risk column is a designer's judgment. The open questions to put to a panel are G5 (slingshot or Y?), G2 (gender symbol?) and G4 against G7 (molecule?).

G1 family

Locked children: angle brackets (graphty-element), the chip (webgpu-graph-algorithms), the page (graph-io), square brackets (graph-format), stacked cards (graph-samples). New ideas for the two open children: layout L1 nodes placed on a grid of positions, L2 the graph on x/y axes, L3 the graph on a circle (circular layout), L4 a corner arrow for "moved into place"; algorithms A1 the route an algorithm found lit in the accent with the rest thinned, A2 the graph in round brackets like a function call f( ), A3 both.
