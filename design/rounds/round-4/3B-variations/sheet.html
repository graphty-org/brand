3B, the zigzag g: refinements, then palettes x fonts

Part 1 compares round 3's 3B with two refinements. Part 2 sets the lockup in the owner's three palettes and six fonts: the three picked fonts (Figtree, Onest, Outfit), Source Sans 3 and DM Sans for their looping two-story g, and Outfit again with its built-in alternate g (stylistic set 1), which is a looping g on otherwise simple geometric letters. Part 2 uses R1 lighter, opened so the favicon gap problem is fixed in every lockup; the original 3B lockups can be rebuilt with BASE_MARK=3B. Every wordmark is the real font converted to outlines.

1. 3B and two refinements

3B3B original

Round 3 as drawn. Rings 7 wide, gap between rings 1.7 units (0.4 px at 16 px), so they touch in the favicon. Gap between rings: 1.73 units of 64.

Rows: ink + lime, graphite + magenta, indigo + amber, one color. The pixelated squares are the real 16 px PNG enlarged 4x.

R1R1 lighter, opened

Rings 6 wide (edges 5.5), both rings 0.5 smaller and the loop pulled 1 down and 1 left: the gap between rings is about 5 units (1.3 px at 16 px), so the middle edge survives. Gap between rings: 5.96 units of 64.

Rows: ink + lime, graphite + magenta, indigo + amber, one color. The pixelated squares are the real 16 px PNG enlarged 4x.

R2R2 steeper ear, bigger node

R1's rings with the ear raised from 22 to 32 degrees and the accent node from r 5.5 to r 6.75, so the node clears the bowl at 16 px and reads as the focus. Gap between rings: 4.85 units of 64.

Rows: ink + lime, graphite + magenta, indigo + amber, one color. The pixelated squares are the real 16 px PNG enlarged 4x.
