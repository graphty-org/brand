Fonts with a looping g

Every font here keeps plain, simple letters but has a double-story ("looping") g, so the g in the word repeats the shape of the 3B mark. Each one is shown next to the 3B mark in the three palettes the owner picked (ink + lime, also on dark; graphite + magenta; indigo + amber), in plain black, and at 20 and 15 px. All are open-licensed (SIL Open Font License 1.1) and are drawn with the real font files.

Recommendation. 1: Outfit with stylistic set 1. It is one of the owner's three favorite fonts; that one switch replaces its round g with a looping g that has a diagonal ear and neck and two near-circle rings, which is very close to the 3B mark. 2: DM Sans. The owner named it, it needs no switch, and its diagonal neck repeats the mark's zigzag. For a retro or art-deco feel, Lexend Exa with stylistic set 1 (number 4) gets there by width and spacing alone, with no decorative letters. Figtree has no looping-g alternate. Onest's alternate g is still single-story.

The g of each font beside the 3B mark
1Outfit + ss01Recommended 1

g: double-story (ss01): round bowl, diagonal ear, diagonal neck, round loop. License: SIL OFL 1.1. CSS: font-weight:500; font-feature-settings:'ss01' 1

The owner's font with a stylistic set that swaps its single-story g for a looped one; the rest stays geometric and plain. The closest echo of the 3B mark: the ear and the neck are diagonals, like the mark's two edges, and both rings are near-circles.
