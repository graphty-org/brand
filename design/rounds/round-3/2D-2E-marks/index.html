Round 3: the depth pyramid and the triangle

Three marks: 2D's pyramid with higher contrast and two back edges; 2E's triangle as it was; and 2E with 2H's depth added. Each has the same family of eight packages. Package marks reuse 2A's brackets (graphty-element), chip (webgpu-graph-algorithms) and file (graph-io); layout and algorithms are 2D's children; graph-format and graph-samples follow 2C, where the amber node turns into the package's icon (a grid of cells, a die for seeded random graphs). cytoscape-extensions is a co-brand lockup with Cytoscape's own logo, unmodified. Wordmark font: Figtree (SIL Open Font License), with two alternatives compared at the end.

2D pyramid, two back edges

Four nodes as a tetrahedron. The back layer is down to two edges (a V through the far node), and the layers are split by contrast as well as size: the three edges that reach toward you are ink, the back layer is indigo, the focus node is amber. On dark the front turns near-white. Edges taper toward the far node.

2E triangle with depth

The same triangle with 2H's sense of depth and none of its parts: the nodes shrink with distance (12, 6.6, 3.6), the edges taper from the focus node outward, and the far edge drops back to indigo while the near edges are ink.

Wordmark fonts

Three simple, open-licensed sans serifs (all SIL Open Font License, from Google Fonts), each with a round single-storey g and a straight-tailed y: none has the sharp corners you disliked in Space Grotesk. Figtree is the closest in spirit to Amazon Ember.
