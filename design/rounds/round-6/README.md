Everything but two choices was locked: the mark (R1), the wordmark (Outfit 500, `ss01` on
"graphty" only), layout (the selection box), algorithms (the traced path and the halo), graph-samples
(the stack of cards) and the heart co-brand. New color rule: "graphty" is always in the ink color
and the package name is in the palette's accent. Left open: which circle carries the color, and
the palette.

## Package names in the accent

Text needs 4.5:1, so where an accent is too faint as text the package name used a darker step of
the same hue on white (lime `#588300`, amber `#b36200`) or a lighter one on dark (magenta
`#ec3f7e`).

## The owner's feedback

- The circle is not picked yet.
- The choice is between green and pink. The green (`#6f9c00`, and the olive text step) is too
  dark: go back to the brighter lime, with several greens if contrast on white and black needs it.
- Figma Buzz uses its logo's accent as the app's accent too: show the graphty app with pink and
  with green as its accent.
- Think about complementary or contrasting colors for a brand palette, and about using another
  palette color in the app if pink or green is too bright there.
