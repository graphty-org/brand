# Decision log

Every choice behind the graphty brand, with what was chosen, what else was considered and where it
lives, when it was decided, the owner's words and the reason. Option labels (2A, R1, N1, G-W5 and so
on) are the labels the round pages used, so each can be found on its page. The owner's full replies
are in [questions.md](questions.md); how to switch the kit to an alternative is in
[README.md](README.md#how-to-revert-or-revisit).

A decision marked **studio** was made by the design studio and shown to the owner without an
objection, rather than chosen by the owner in words.
